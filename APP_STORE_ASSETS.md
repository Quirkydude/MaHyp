## PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated: [Date]**

### Introduction
MaHyp ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application MaHyp.

### Information We Collect

#### Personal Information
- **Name**: To personalize your experience
- **Email Address**: For account creation and support
- **Phone Number**: For account verification via OTP
- **Date of Birth**: To provide age-appropriate health insights

#### Health Information
- **Blood Pressure Readings**: Systolic and diastolic values, heart rate, timestamps
- **Medication Information**: Medication names, dosages, schedules, adherence logs
- **Symptoms**: Dizziness, headaches, nausea (if you choose to log them)
- **Notes**: Optional notes about your health

#### Usage Information
- App features you use
- Time spent in app
- Device type and operating system
- Crash reports and performance data

### How We Use Your Information

1. **Provide Core Services**:
   - Store and display your blood pressure readings
   - Manage your medication schedules and reminders
   - Generate health insights and reports

2. **Improve Our App**:
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
   - Fix bugs and optimize performance
   - Develop new features

3. **Communication**:
   - Send important account notifications
   - Respond to your support requests
   - Send medication reminders (if enabled)

4. **Security**:
   - Verify your identity via phone OTP
   - Protect against unauthorized access
   - Monitor for suspicious activity

### Data Storage and Security

#### Storage
- Your data is stored on **Google Firebase** (Firestore database)
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Data is encrypted at rest (Firebase security)
- OTP codes are temporary and expire after 5 minutes

#### Security Measures
- Secure authentication (phone verification)
- Encrypted data transmission
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

### Data Sharing

#### We DO NOT sell your data.

#### We MAY share your data with:
1. **Service Providers**:
   - Firebase (Google) - for database and authentication
   - Arkesel SMS - for OTP verification only
   - Analytics services (anonymized data)

2. **Legal Requirements**:
   - If required by law or legal process
   - To protect our rights or safety
   - To prevent fraud or security issues

3. **With Your Consent**:
   - If you explicitly authorize sharing
   - For specific purposes you approve

### Your Rights

#### Access and Control
- View all your data in the app
- Edit your profile information
- Export your health data
- Delete your account and all data

#### Account Deletion
You can delete your account at any time:
1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
2. Or email us at: your-support@email.com
3. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

#### Data Portability
- Export your blood pressure history as PDF
- Export medication logs
- Download all your data in JSON format

### Children's Privacy
MaHyp is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

### Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new policy in the app
- Sending an email notification
- Updating the "Last Updated" date

## 🗑️ Account Deletion Information

### For Account Deletion Page in App:

```
DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

We're sorry to see you go, but we respect your decision.

What happens when you delete your account:
✓ All your blood pressure readings will be permanently deleted
✓ All medication data and schedules will be removed
✓ Your profile information will be erased
✓ All reminders and notifications will be stopped
✓ Your account will be permanently closed

What you should know:
• This action cannot be undone
• Data deletion is permanent and irreversible
• It may take up to 30 days to complete
• You will receive a confirmation email
• You can re-register with the same phone number if needed

Before you delete:
• Export any data you want to keep (Settings → Export Data)
• Cancel any upcoming medication reminders
• Save important health reports

To delete your account:

Option 1: In-App Deletion
1. Go to Settings
2. Tap "Account Settings"
3. Select "Delete Account"
4. Confirm your decision
5. Enter your phone number for verification
6. Tap "Delete My Account"

Option 2: Email Request
Send an email to: your-support@email.com
Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
Include:
- Your full name
- Phone number used for registration
- Email address (if any)
- Reason for leaving (optional)

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

Questions? Contact us at your-support@email.com
```
